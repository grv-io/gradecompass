GradeCompass

Stainless grade selection worked backwards from the environment: location and application in — grade, elimination trail and cost per service-year out.

1Fingerprint the environment. The district gives an ISO 9223-style corrosivity class; chemical and food-contact uses follow their own service pathway.
2Failure modes veto grades. Pitting floor, service temperature, weld sensitisation, microbial attack and hydrogen embrittlement eliminate — a range class eliminates on its lower bound and warns on its upper.

PROTOTYPE · Assumptions & limitations
click to expand

  

    

      
Subset scope 12 grades · 12 districts · 7 applications
        
The production build covers Jindal's full catalogue (120+ grades) and all ~800 Indian districts with the same engine logic. Nothing here is a Jindal proprietary grade; production loads them from mill test certificates.

      
Curated candidate lists tags do most of the narrowing at this size
        
A query only considers grades tagged as relevant to that application, so elimination chooses among 2–5 candidates and the tag list, not the failure-mode rules, does most of the narrowing. With the full catalogue the rules carry far more of the decision.

      
Hand-compiled environment data approximate normals, not a pipeline
        
Each district's fingerprint (distance to coast from the city centre, day-mean RH, annual rainfall, an SO₂ level flag) was curated from published climate records and is boundary-sensitive. Production computes these from IMD, ERA5, CPCB and CSIR-CECRI data.

      
Proxy corrosivity classifier six rules, not ISO 9223 dose-response
        
The class is an ISO 9223-style proxy from a six-rule set on coast, humidity, rainfall and SO₂; ISO 9223 itself has no hybrid classes and derives C1–CX from time-of-wetness, SO₂ and chloride deposition. Within 5 km of the coast SO₂ is not used, so Visakhapatnam and Goa share a class, and no district in this subset reaches C5-plus or CX. Sheltered and washed exposures are not distinguished.

      
Partial failure-mode registry 5 active, 4 registered
        
Active: pitting floor, service temperature, weld sensitisation (300-series non-L grades only; 202 and 430 weld-zone effects are not yet modelled), microbial (MIC) screening and a hydrogen-embrittlement family exclusion. Stress-corrosion cracking, crevice, galvanic and erosion–corrosion are registered but not enforced.

      
Indicative economics ex-mill ratios · engineering-judgment life bands
        
₹/kg uses a fixed ₹175 baseline for grade 430 with indicative ex-mill cost ratios, not live market or Stainless Mart prices. Domestic stockist quotes for thinly traded grades run higher (2205 ≈ ₹450–650/kg against ₹315 here), which is why the table lets you overwrite any price and shows the break-even. Service life is a four-band engineering-judgment estimate from PREN margin, applied only to atmospheric exposure; no ISO service-life model exists for stainless steel (ISO 9224 covers carbon steel, zinc, copper and aluminium). Maintenance regime is not an input. Treat ₹/kg-year as directional, not quotable.

      
Nominal-composition properties not per-heat data
        
PREN is computed at nominal ASTM A240 composition (202 deliberately at the low end to cover Indian Cr–Mn variants); yield strengths are A240 minima; temperature limits are the lower of published continuous/intermittent figures. PREN is a comparative screening index — real performance also depends on finish, crevice geometry, temperature and maintenance.

      
Unvalidated ranking weights judgment, pending panel calibration
        
Scoring weights, the marginal penalty and the cost-sensitivity mapping are engineering judgment; about a quarter of all input combinations are close calls (top-two gap under 0.03), which the page now says out loud. The production build calibrates against a 200+ scenario suite scored by a Jindal metallurgist panel (target ≥ 95% top-3 agreement).

      
No certification checks yet BIS / ASME / RDSO as tags only
        
Compliance appears only as curated application tags; production validates against a certification registry per grade and product form.

      
Demonstration architecture engine inlined in this page
        
The engine runs in the browser for the demo and returns the same payload the production API will serve from a governed backend, with grade data maintained by Jindal's quality team through an admin interface. Escalation here opens your mail client with the record pre-filled; production routes it to the technical advisory queue.
