PROTOTYPE · Assumptions & limitations
Subset scope This prototype runs on 12 grades, 12 districts and 7 applications. The production build covers Jindal's full catalogue (120+ grades) and ~740 Indian districts, with the same engine logic unchanged.
Hand-compiled environment data Each district's fingerprint (coastal distance, humidity, rainfall, SO₂) was curated from published climate and corrosion records. Production computes these from IMD, ERA5, CPCB and CSIR-CECRI data pipelines.
Simplified corrosivity classifier The ISO 9223 class comes from a reduced rule set. Production uses the full time-of-wetness and chloride-deposition dose–response calculation, calibrated against CSIR-CECRI exposure-station data.
Curated candidate lists A query only considers grades tagged as relevant to that application, so elimination here chooses among 2–4 candidates. With the full catalogue, the failure-mode rules carry much more of the decision.
Partial failure-mode registry Active rules: pitting floor, service temperature, weld sensitization, microbial (MIC) risk and a hydrogen-embrittlement family exclusion (duplex not offered for H₂-wetted service). Stress-corrosion cracking, crevice, galvanic and erosion–corrosion are registered for the full build but not yet enforced here.
Indicative economics ₹/kg uses a fixed ₹175 baseline for grade 430 with indicative ex-mill cost ratios — not live market or Stainless Mart prices; domestic stockist quotes for thinly traded grades such as 2205 run well above the ratio used here (₹450–650/kg vs ₹315), which narrows but does not reverse the per-year inversion. Service life is a four-band engineering-judgment estimate from PREN margin — no ISO service-life model exists for stainless steel (ISO 9224 covers only carbon steel, zinc, copper and aluminium); treat ₹/kg-year as directional, not quotable.
Nominal-composition properties PREN is computed at nominal ASTM A240 composition and cross-checked against the IMOA/ISSF PREN table; yield strengths are A240 minima; temperature limits are the lower of published continuous/intermittent figures — none of it is per-heat mill test certificate data. PREN itself is a comparative screening index — actual performance also depends on surface finish, crevice geometry, temperature and maintenance.
Unvalidated ranking weights The scoring weights and cost-sensitivity mapping are engineering judgment. Round 2 calibrates them against a 200+ scenario suite scored by a Jindal metallurgist panel (target ≥95% top-3 agreement).
No certification checks yet BIS, ASME and RDSO compliance appear only as curated application tags; the production build validates recommendations against the certification registry per grade and product form.
Demonstration architecture The engine runs inside this page for the demo. In production the identical logic is served from a governed backend, with grade data maintained by Jindal's quality team through an admin interface.

PREN = %Cr + 3.3 × %Mo + 16 × %N — a comparative pitting-resistance index, not a guarantee of corrosion immunity. Prices are indicative (₹175/kg at the 430 baseline); lifetime bands are engineering-judgment estimates, not a standards model. For critical applications, confirm with Jindal's technical advisory team.
